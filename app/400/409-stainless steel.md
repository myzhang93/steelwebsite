
# 409 Stainless Steel – Titanium-Stabilised Ferritic Exhaust Champion for 815 °C Performance at Automotive Cost

409 Stainless Steel is the titanium-stabilised ferritic grade purpose-built for the punishing thermal cycles, acid condensates and vibration found in modern exhaust systems. With 10.5–11.75 % chromium, ≤0.08 % carbon and titanium ≥6× carbon content, UNS S40900 forms a stable, self-healing oxide that survives continuous 815 °C while remaining readily deep-drawn, magnetically attractive and weldable without post-weld anneal. ## Key Features & Benefits – Why 409 Stainless Steel Thrives in Hot Gas, Condensate & Cyclic Heat

### Titanium Stabilisation = Zero Sensitisation
- TiC formation locks up carbon, preventing chromium depletion along weld HAZ; as-welded joints pass ASTM A262 Practice E without fissures.
- No post-weld anneal required—saves furnace energy and distortion rework on large exhaust manifolds.

### Good High-Temperature Oxidation Resistance
- Parabolic scaling rate < 0.05 mm/year at 800 °C—four times better than aluminised steel and half of 430, extending muffler life to 10 years/240 000 km.

### Excellent Exhaust-Gas & Condensate Corrosion Resistance
- 11 % Cr passive film resists sulfuric and nitric acids formed when exhaust cools below 150 °C; ASTM G85 acetic-acid-salt-spray > 500 h with <2 % red rust.

### Superior Formability & Deep Drawability
- Low work-hardening exponent (n ≈ 0.18) allows 55 % deep-draw ratios in converter shells, eliminating intermediate anneals during multi-stage pressing.

### Low Thermal Expansion & Higher Conductivity
- Coefficient 11.5 µm·m⁻¹·K⁻¹ (20–800 °C) reduces thermal fatigue when 409 expands against 1020 steel flanges; conductivity 24 W·m⁻¹·K⁻¹ speeds catalyst light-off.

### Magnetic & Non-Galling
- Ferromagnetic structure suits induction welding and magnetic clamping; no nickel means no austenite → martensite shift during thermal cycling.

### Cost-Effective & Price-Stable
- Nickel-free chemistry tracks only chrome price; buyers typically save USD 300–400 per tonne versus 304 while meeting 90 % of exhaust corrosion requirements.

## Manufacturing Process & Quality Assurance

We melt 409 Stainless Steel in an electric-arc furnace, argon-oxygen decarburise to ≤0.03 % C, then add titanium wire to achieve 6× C minimum (typically 0.2 % Ti). Continuous casting into 200 mm slabs is followed by hot rolling to 3 mm coil and cold rolling to final gauge. Continuous annealing at 800–900 °C in 100 % hydrogen produces a clean, oxide-free surface with optimum ductility. Finishes include 2D for general exhaust pipe, 2B for decorative trim, BA bright anneal and No. 4 satin. Every coil is certified to ASTM A240/A268, ISO 9001 and EN 10204 3.1; tests include ICP chemistry, elevated-temperature tensile at 700 °C, ASTM G85 cyclic corrosion in synthetic condensate, and magnetic permeability (< 1.3). Third-party inspection certificates and REACH/RoHS compliance statements are available for automotive OEMs.

## Maintenance & Care Tips

Clean 409 Stainless Steel with warm water and a mild, chloride-free detergent; rinse and dry to prevent water spots. For exhaust components, annual inspection for oxide spallation is recommended—light brushing and compressed-air blowing restore emissivity. Avoid hydrochloric or ferric-chloride cleaners that can initiate pitting. Store coil indoors, vertically separated by plastic, to prevent carbon-steel contact and subsequent rust staining.
