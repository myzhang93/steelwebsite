## Key Features & Benefits – Why 204 Stainless Steel Outperforms Its Price Tag

### Mechanical Muscle
- Tensile strength ≥ 650 MPa – down-gauge 0.15 mm vs 304 and still exceed design loads.
- 45 % elongation enables aggressive deep draws, tight brake-form radii and complex stamping without tearing—ideal for sink bowls or muffler shells.

### Corrosion & Oxidation Resistance
- 16 % Cr + nitrogen create a self-healing passive film; ASTM B117 salt-spray data show 204 Stainless Steel survives 500 h with &lt;3 % red-rust area, outperforming 201 by 40 %.
- Stable austenite from –40 °C to 850 °C—perfect for outdoor roofing, exhaust systems and cryogenic tie-rods.

### Formability & Deep Drawability
- Lower work-hardening exponent (n ≈ 0.35) than 301 or 304; reduces press tonnage and intermediate anneals, saving hours on the shop floor.
- Excellent arc-weldability with 308L or 204 filler—no pre-heat, no post-weld stress relief, lowering fabrication cost.

### Cost & Sustainability Edge
- Reduced nickel content lowers alloy surcharge; buyers locked into annual contracts often pocket USD 120–180 per tonne saving over 304.
- 100 % recyclable, RoHS compliant and free of restricted heavy metals—helps earn LEED MR credits.

## Maintenance & Care Tips

Clean 204 Stainless Steel with mild detergent and warm water; avoid chloride bleach. Rinse welds and corners, then wipe dry to prevent water spots. For exterior panels, an annual wipe-down with 1 % nitric acid restores the passive film and keeps tea-staining at bay. Store sheets vertically on plastic-covered racks to avoid carbon-steel contamination.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** Is 204 Stainless Steel magnetic?  
**A:** Annealed 204 is non-magnetic; cold-worked areas may pick up slight magnetism, but values remain &lt;2 G, suitable for electronics housings.

**Q:** How does 204 compare to 304 in cost and performance?  
**A:** In mild urban or rural atmospheres, 204 delivers comparable corrosion resistance at 8–10 % lower cost; upgrade to 304 for coastal or chemical duty.

**Q:** Can I polish 204 to a mirror?  
**A:** Absolutely—start with 180 grit and step to 1 000, then green compound; BA substrate achieves 500 gloss units, equal to 304.
