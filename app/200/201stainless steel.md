## Manufacturing Process and Quality Assurance

201 Stainless Steel is produced via electric-arc furnace melting, argon-oxygen decarburisation and continuous casting into 200 mm slabs. Hot rolling to 2 mm coil is followed by cold rolling to final gauge. Full annealing at 1010 °C in hydrogen atmosphere produces ultra-smooth 2B finish. Finishes include 2B for general forming, BA for mirror reflectivity, and No. 4 satin for decorative panels. Every coil is certified to ASTM A240, ISO 9001 and EN 10204 3.1; tests include ICP chemistry, mechanical properties per ASTM A240, and dimensional tolerance to ASTM A480. Third-party inspection certificates (TÜV, Lloyd’s) are available on request.

## Maintenance and Care Tips for 201 Stainless Steel

Clean with warm water and neutral detergent; rinse and dry to prevent water spots. Avoid prolonged contact with chloride bleach or hydrochloric acid. For coastal installations, rinse with fresh water after storms. Store indoors on plastic-separated racks to prevent carbon-steel contamination.
